# Quick start

1. Install Lumora and open it. Choose a display name. Optionally set a passphrase (strongest protection).
2. **Write down your recovery phrase** — 24 English words, in order. It is the only way to restore your identity on a new device. Lumora then asks for 3 of the words to check your copy. (A code from an older version, groups like `abcde-fgh2j-…`, still works.)
3. Share your **9-digit ID** (or QR code) with friends; add theirs under *Friends → Add*.
4. Chat, call, send files of any size. Everything is end-to-end encrypted and goes directly device-to-device.
5. **Remote desktop:** give your partner your ID + the temporary password shown on the *Remote* tab.
   You will be asked to accept. On Linux Wayland see [permissions](wayland.md).
